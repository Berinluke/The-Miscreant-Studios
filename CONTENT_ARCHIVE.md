### The 4 Non-Negotiable Principles
1. **CREATE — No Safe Ideas:** If a game concept doesn't feel slightly dangerous or computationally insane, it is simply not ambitious enough for our studio.

## 8. TECHNICAL ARSENAL & ENGINE STACK

### 1. Unity 6 & DOTS
- High-concurrency mobile & desktop architecture utilizing Data-Oriented Technology Stack (DOTS) and Universal Render Pipeline (URP).
- WebGPU Engine compilation and adaptive dynamic performance scaling.
- Capable of simulating 64K+ concurrent entity burst threads with `< 2ms` frame-time.

### 2. Bare-Metal C++ & C#
- Zero-cost abstractions, custom memory arenas, and SIMD parallel computations running directly on native silicon for uncompromising performance.
- AVX-512 vectorization, lock-free queues, cache-conscious locality, and 0.4ms physics tick steps.

### 3. Unreal Engine 5.5
- Nanite virtualized micro-polygon geometry.
- Lumen real-time dynamic global illumination and reflections.
- Chaos physics destruction and Niagara GPU particle simulations.

### 4. Digital Content Creation (DCC) Pipeline
- **Blender 4.x:** Hard-surface geometry, custom rigging, procedural geometry nodes.
- **Houdini FX:** Procedural world generation, destruction simulations, pyro effects.
- **Wwise / FMOD:** Spatial audio DSP, custom binaural filters, dynamic interactive score sequencing.
