02 • Studio Culture & Non-Negotiable Pillars

01. CREATE — No Safe Ideas

If a game concept doesn't feel slightly dangerous or computationally insane, it is simply not ambitious enough for our studio.

02. EXPERIMENT — Prototype Relentlessly

Every Friday our engineers and artists test wild gameplay loops. 90% are discarded, but the 10% that survive define our games.

03. BREAK — Stress-Test Past the Redline

We intentionally overload UDP packets and trigger 50,000 physics fragments to find every breaking point before players do.

04. REBUILD — Polish into Obsidian

We rebuild the survivors with ruthless care, polishing micro-animations, audio punch, and 120 FPS consistency until it's undeniable.
